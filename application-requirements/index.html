Application Requirements

Thank you for your interest in Spradley Properties. We look forward to helping you find your next home!

To ensure a smooth application process, please review the information below.
Prospective tenants can find important details in the Information About Brokerage Services (PDF).
General Disclaimer
    • Per our contractual obligation to our rental property owners, all rental properties listed will remain marketed as available until a lease on the property is signed. We process applications on a first-come, first-served basis. Please note that we often receive multiple applications for the same property, and other applications may have been submitted and are being actively processed before yours.
    • All fully submitted applications are valid for 30 days after the submission date.
    • If you have a housing voucher, please use our website to verify that a property is Section 8 approved before submitting an application.
    • Application fees are non-refundable. Our nationwide screening partner electronically processes credit and criminal background checks immediately upon payment and submission.
If you have any questions regarding our application process, please contact our office before submitting your online application. You can contact us during business hours (M-F, 9 AM-5 PM CST) at 254-742-7733 or email leasing@spradleyproperties.com. Please be aware that we cannot prequalify applicants over the phone.

Privacy Policy

We are dedicated to protecting the privacy of your personal information, including your Social Security Number and other identifying details. Our policy and procedures are designed to ensure that your information remains secure. We use the information provided in your application solely for business purposes related to leasing a home to you, such as verifying application statements, reviewing lease renewals, and enforcing lease obligations. Once your personal information is no longer required, it will be properly disposed of. 

Required Application Documents

All adult residents (18 years or older) and/or guarantors must provide the following items with their application:
  • Valid government-issued photo ID (e.g., driver’s license, state ID) for each adult applicant/guarantor.
  • Past 3 months’ pay stubs for each adult applicant/guarantor.
  • Section 8 voucher (if applicable).
  • If self-employed: Two years of tax returns.
  • If relocating or starting new employment: An employment contract, written job offer, or letter of transfer (on company letterhead) from the new employer, including salary and hire/start date.
  • If retired: Most recent SSI, VA Benefits, and/or Retirement Benefits statements; OR the past 3 months’ bank statement showing these deposits.
  • A $50 non-refundable application fee per adult applicant/guarantor.
  • Proof of coverage for the duration of the lease is required before keys are released. All residents are required to maintain a $100,000 minimum liability protection with Spradley Properties listed as an additional interest.

 

Eligibility Criteria

  • Household monthly income must be verifiable. The total income of all applicants must be 2.5 times the monthly rent.
  • Applicants who do not have sufficient income may qualify by having the lease guaranteed by one or more guarantors.
    • Guarantors may be family members or employers.
    • At least one guarantor must have a gross monthly income of at least 4 times the monthly rent.
    • All guarantors must meet all other qualifying criteria.
    • The guarantor(s) must complete and sign the lease.
    • Note: All guarantors are guaranteeing the lease contract and not an individual applicant.
  • The family/occupant size must be appropriate for the available dwelling. The maximum occupancy is two people per bedroom.
  • Single family dwellings and duplexes are restricted to no more than four adults per home.
  • Credit screening is conducted through AmRent using data from a VantageScore® Score Model; credit scores below 600 are not qualified. Credit scores affected by medical bills or student loans may be considered, and approval is based on overall credit profile, not solely the score. Click here to learn more about why credit scores may differ.
  • Applicants must review our pet policy and restricted breed list.

Spradley Properties does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, creed, religion, sex, national origin, disability, or familial status.

Applicant(s) may be denied occupancy for the following reasons:
  • Falsification of application or misleading information by any applicant.
  • Insufficient income.
  • Criminal conviction history committed by any applicant or by other occupants.
  • Poor credit history of any applicant.
  • Large outstanding balances.
  • History of bankruptcy.
    • Applicants with cases of bankruptcy from more than 3 years ago may still be considered eligible.
  • Poor rental profile including:
    • Rental debt.
    • Non-payment of rent.
    • Frequent late payments.
    • Eviction.
    • Poor housekeeping.
    • Unruly or destructive behavior by the applicant, applicant’s occupants, or applicant’s guests.
    • Violence to persons or property by the applicant, applicant’s occupants, or applicant’s guests.

All applications are eligible for appeal within 48 hours of a final decision. Please refer to our Application Denial Appeal Policy for further details. If a denial is upheld, applicants must wait 60 days before reapplying.

Pet Policy

Many of our properties are pet friendly; however, with the exception of verified assistance animals, all pets are subject to approval by the property owner.  Pet policies vary from one owner to another.  Some owners do not allow pets, while others restrict quantity, size, type, age etc.  Please check with your leasing consultant about the property you are interested in before you submit and pay the non-refundable application fee.
Many city ordinances restrict the number of pets, and typically no more than two animals are allowed per home.  Acceptable pets may include: dogs, cats, small caged animals, caged birds, and fish. All pets are subject to deposits, non-refundable fees, and monthly pet rent.

Pet restricted breed list includes but is not limited to:
Staffordshire Terriers, American Pit Bull Terriers, Staffordshire Bull Terriers, Doberman Pinschers, Rottweilers, Chow Chows, Presa Canarios, Dalmatians, American Bulldogs, Cane Corso, Mastiffs, German Shepherds, Wolf Hybrids, or any mix of the above. If you are unsure if your pet is a restricted breed, please contact Spradley Property Management for assistance, or see this Restricted Breed List (PDF) for more information.

Application Process

The application process generally takes 2-4 business days and is contingent on how quickly we receive needed information.
If approved, you will be notified via email/text. You will receive an approval letter that outlines lease terms and move-in charges. Upon receipt of the lease, all occupants will have 48 hours to sign. Due to the high demand for rental properties, we are unable to hold the property off the rental market for more than two (2) business days without a signed lease.
Please be aware that the majority of our homes require enrollment in our Resident Benefit Program at an additional cost in addition to monthly rent. We can hold a vacant home up to 14 days after a lease has been signed by all parties. To avoid potential conflict caused by time constraints, please ensure that your desired move-in date matches the availability date.

We look forward to helping you find your next home!

If you have any questions/concerns regarding the application process or rental criteria, please call us at 254-742-7733 or email leasing@spradleyproperties.com.
