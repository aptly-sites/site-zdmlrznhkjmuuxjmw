How do I apply for a home?+

Choose a home from our available listings and select Apply now to begin your application through Rent Manager. Review our application requirements before applying.

How can I schedule a tour?+

Contact our leasing team at 254.742.7733 or leasing@spradleyproperties.com. Tell us which property you would like to see.

Where can I pay rent or request maintenance?+

Use the Resident Portal to view your account, pay rent, or submit routine maintenance requests. For an urgent maintenance issue, call 254.742.7733.

What types of properties do you manage?+

Our services cover residential homes, multifamily communities, and commercial properties in Central Texas. Contact us to discuss your property.
