SMS Text Messaging Terms of Service

1Program Description

Spradley Properties operates an SMS text messaging program to communicate with owners, tenants, and prospective clients regarding property management services, leasing inquiries, maintenance updates, and general real estate information. Messages may include service updates, appointment reminders, marketing communications, and responses to your inquiries.

2How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Spradley Properties by:

  • Submitting your phone number and consent through the contact form on our website at www.spradleyproperties.com
  • Texting START to 254-276-5770
  • Providing written or verbal consent to a Spradley Properties representative

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Spradley Properties at the mobile number provided.

3Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions and inquiries with Spradley Properties. You may receive up to 4 messages per month. Additional messages may be sent in response to specific requests or time-sensitive property matters.

4Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. These charges are billed by and payable to your mobile carrier. Spradley Properties is not responsible for any charges from your wireless carrier.

5How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages from Spradley Properties at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us. After texting STOP, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.

Additional opt-out keywords: END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT — any of these will remove you from our messaging list.

6Help and Support

For help, reply HELP to any of our text messages or contact us directly:

7Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Information collected through this SMS program, including your phone number, will be used solely to communicate with you regarding Spradley Properties services. No mobile information will be shared with third party/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. For complete details, please review our Data Privacy Policy at:

www.spradleyproperties.com/data-privacy-page/

Spradley Properties SMS messages are supported by major U.S. wireless carriers including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, and others. Carrier support may vary and is not guaranteed on all networks. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
