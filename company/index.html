Spradley Properties began in Temple in 2005. Today, our team brings together brokerage, property management, and maintenance services for residential and commercial properties.

Our office on North 31st Street is a place to connect with the people behind your property. We value dependable service, thoughtful communication, and care for the places entrusted to us.
